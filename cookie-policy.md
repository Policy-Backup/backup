# TorBox Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how TorBox uses cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies when you access or use TorBox, TorBox.App, our websites, dashboard, applications, APIs, software, tools, bots, integrations, support services, and related products and services (collectively, the “**Service**”).

This Cookie Policy should be read together with the TorBox Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and other applicable policies.

## 1. What Cookies and Similar Technologies Are

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your browser or device. Cookies can help websites operate, keep you logged in, remember settings, secure accounts, understand usage, and provide features.

TorBox may also use similar technologies, including:

1. local storage;
2. session storage;
3. pixels;
4. SDKs;
5. device identifiers;
6. authentication tokens;
7. session identifiers;
8. API-related identifiers;
9. security tokens; and
10. similar tracking, storage, or identification technologies.

In this Cookie Policy, we refer to these technologies collectively as “**Cookies**.”

## 2. How TorBox Uses Cookies

TorBox may use Cookies for the following purposes:

1. to operate the Service;
2. to keep you logged in;
3. to authenticate users;
4. to remember account settings and preferences;
5. to secure accounts and prevent fraud;
6. to detect abuse, security incidents, and unauthorized activity;
7. to support payments, subscriptions, vouchers, and checkout flows;
8. to support support widgets, help center tools, changelog tools, and feature-request tools;
9. to understand how users interact with the Service;
10. to diagnose errors and improve performance;
11. to route traffic, improve reliability, and protect infrastructure;
12. to support integrations and connected services; and
13. to comply with legal, security, operational, and policy-enforcement requirements.

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

**3.1 Essential Cookies**

Essential Cookies are necessary for the Service to work. They may be used for login, authentication, account security, session management, fraud prevention, payment processing, load balancing, infrastructure protection, API security, and similar functions.

Without these Cookies, parts of the Service may not function properly.

Examples may include Cookies or similar technologies used by TorBox, Cloudflare, Supabase, payment providers, authentication providers, or other infrastructure and security providers.

**3.2 Preference Cookies**

Preference Cookies help remember choices you make, such as account settings, interface preferences, language preferences, dashboard settings, or other user-selected options.

These Cookies help improve your experience but are not always strictly necessary for the Service to operate.

**3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies**

Analytics and performance Cookies help us understand how users interact with the Service, diagnose technical issues, measure performance, and improve features.

TorBox may use privacy-focused analytics providers, including SimpleAnalytics. Analytics information may include device model, browser type, approximate location or referral information, pages viewed, feature usage, and aggregate usage metrics.

TorBox does not use analytics Cookies to sell personal information for money or to share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, unless we update our Privacy Policy and provide any required notices and choices.

**3.4 Support and Product Feedback Cookies**

TorBox may use Cookies and similar technologies to support help center tools, support widgets, chat tools, feature-request tools, changelog widgets, product feedback tools, and related support functions.

These tools may be provided by third-party providers such as Intercom or Featurebase. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

**3.5 Payment and Checkout Cookies**

TorBox may use Cookies and similar technologies to support checkout, subscriptions, payment processing, fraud prevention, billing, vouchers, refunds, chargebacks, and related payment functions.

These tools may be provided by third-party payment providers, subscription billing providers, payment orchestration providers, banks, card networks, wallet providers, resellers, or other payment-related providers, including providers such as Spreedly and Chargebee.

TorBox does not store full payment card numbers on its servers.

**3.6 Authentication Cookies**

If you sign in using a third-party login provider, such as Google, Microsoft, Apple, or another OAuth provider, that provider may use Cookies or similar technologies to authenticate you, secure your login, and operate its services.

Your use of third-party authentication services is subject to the privacy policies and cookie practices of those providers.

**3.7 Marketing Cookies**

TorBox does not currently intend to use Cookies to sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If TorBox later uses advertising, retargeting, or marketing Cookies that require consent or opt-out rights, TorBox will update this Cookie Policy and provide any notices and choices required by law.

## 6. How Long Cookies Last

Cookies may be session Cookies or persistent Cookies.

Session Cookies usually expire when you close your browser or end your session.

Persistent Cookies remain on your browser or device for a longer period, unless you delete them or they expire.

The length of time a Cookie remains on your device depends on the purpose of the Cookie, your browser settings, the provider placing the Cookie, and applicable retention settings.
