# TorBox EU Digital Services Act Notice

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This EU Digital Services Act Notice explains how TorBox handles certain notices, communications, and complaints under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, known as the Digital Services Act (“**DSA**”) as well as handling Article 27 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

TorBox provides a cloud-based download-management, caching, retrieval, storage-access, automation, and media-access service. TorBox is not designed as a public content-sharing platform, social network, online marketplace, app store, advertising platform, or public search engine. TorBox is not designed to publish, promote, recommend, or distribute user content to the public.

This Notice supplements the TorBox [Terms of Service](https://torbox.app/terms), Acceptable Use Policy, Copyright / DMCA Policy, Abuse and Illegal Content Reporting Policy, [Privacy Policy](https://torbox.app/privacy), API and Developer Terms, and Law Enforcement Request Policy.

## 4. Reporting Illegal Content or Illegal Activity

Reports of illegal content or illegal activity involving the Service may be submitted to:

[abuse@torbox.app](mailto:abuse@torbox.app)

Copyright complaints must be submitted directly to TorBox’s designated copyright agent under the TorBox Copyright / DMCA Policy at:

[dmca@torbox.app](mailto:dmca@torbox.app)

A copyright complaint submitted through the DSA, abuse, support, legal, community, or another reporting channel is not submitted to TorBox’s designated copyright agent, will not be treated as a DMCA notice, and will not be forwarded. The sender must resubmit the complaint directly to the designated copyright agent.

To help TorBox review a report, please include enough information to identify the material, account, request, or activity at issue. Helpful information may include:

1. the specific reason you believe the material or activity is illegal;
2. the law you believe is being violated, if known;
3. URLs, magnet links, torrent hashes, info hashes, file hashes, filenames, file sizes, NZB information, Usenet information, hoster links, RSS feed information, webhook information, request identifiers, account identifiers, timestamps, screenshots, logs, or other technical identifiers;
4. your name and email address, unless you are submitting an anonymous report where permitted;
5. a statement confirming that you have a good-faith belief that the information and allegations in your report are accurate and complete; and
6. any other information that may help TorBox understand and evaluate the report.

Please do not submit, upload, attach, or transmit illegal material when making a report, including child sexual abuse material or non-consensual intimate imagery. Instead, provide technical identifiers or other information that may help TorBox locate and evaluate the material or activity.

## 5. Review of Notices

TorBox may review notices based on the information provided, applicable law, technical feasibility, operational needs, user safety, service integrity, and TorBox policies.

TorBox may reject, close, or request more information for notices that are incomplete, inaccurate, abusive, fraudulent, duplicative, automated without meaningful review, submitted in bad faith, unrelated to TorBox, or unsupported by enough information to identify the material or activity at issue.

TorBox may take action on a notice even if the notice does not include every item requested in this Notice if TorBox can reasonably identify the material or activity at issue.

## 6. Actions TorBox May Take

TorBox may take any action it determines appropriate in response to illegal content, suspected illegal activity, security issues, fraud, abuse, copyright matters, or policy violations.

Actions may include:

1. removing, blocking, de-caching, disabling access to, or restricting materials, files, hashes, links, torrents, NZBs, hosters, sources, streams, exports, download requests, integrations, or other materials;
2. preventing re-adding, re-caching, or future matching requests for materials;
3. warning users;
4. throttling, rate-limiting, or restricting activity;
5. rotating, revoking, disabling, or restricting API keys, access tokens, webhooks, bots, RSS feeds, or integrations;
6. suspending, downgrading, restricting, or terminating accounts, Plans, paid features, Vouchers, or Service access;
7. preserving information;
8. reporting suspected illegal activity to law enforcement, regulators, child safety organizations, rights holders, payment processors, vendors, infrastructure providers, or other appropriate parties;
9. disclosing information as described in the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Law Enforcement Request Policy, and applicable law; or
10. taking any other action TorBox determines necessary to protect the Service, users, third parties, vendors, payment processors, infrastructure providers, partners, or the public.

## 7. Statement of Reasons

Where required by the DSA, TorBox will provide affected users with a clear and specific statement of reasons when TorBox removes, disables access to, de-caches, blocks, restricts, suspends, terminates, or otherwise takes action regarding material, activity, an account, or Service access based on alleged illegality or violation of TorBox’s Terms or policies.

A statement of reasons may include, where applicable:

1. the action taken;
2. whether the action concerns illegal content or violation of TorBox’s Terms or policies;
3. the facts and circumstances relied on in taking the action;
4. whether automated means were used in making or supporting the decision;
5. the policy or legal basis for the action; and
6. available options for review or appeal, if any.

TorBox is not required to provide information where doing so would violate law, compromise safety or security, disclose confidential information, reveal detection methods, undermine enforcement, interfere with an investigation, disclose another user’s information, or create legal, operational, abuse, or risk-management concerns.

Copyright counter-notices must be submitted under the Copyright / DMCA Policy, not this DSA review process.

## 9. Abuse of Notice or Review Processes

You must not submit false, misleading, abusive, fraudulent, duplicative, automated, harassing, or bad-faith notices or review requests.

TorBox may reject notices or review requests and may restrict reporting or account access if TorBox believes the reporting or review process is being misused.

## 10. Trusted Flaggers

If TorBox receives a notice from an entity formally designated as a trusted flagger under the DSA and the notice relates to a Service function covered by the DSA, TorBox will treat the notice in accordance with applicable DSA requirements.

TorBox may request information reasonably necessary to verify trusted flagger status and to identify the material or activity at issue.

## 11. Orders from EU Authorities

TorBox will review orders from European Union Member State authorities, courts, Digital Services Coordinators, the European Commission, or other competent authorities in accordance with applicable law.

TorBox may request clarification, verification, narrowing, or additional legal process where appropriate.

## 12. Transparency Reporting

Where required by the DSA, TorBox will make available information regarding its content moderation, notice-handling, enforcement, or complaint-handling practices in the manner and at the intervals required by applicable law.

TorBox may include information such as the number and types of notices received, actions taken, orders received from authorities, user review requests, and other information required by the DSA, subject to applicable law, user privacy, security, confidentiality, and operational requirements.

## 13. Terms and Policy Restrictions

TorBox’s Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, Copyright / DMCA Policy, Abuse and Illegal Content Reporting Policy, API and Developer Terms, and other policies describe restrictions that apply to use of the Service.

TorBox may enforce those restrictions using manual review, automated systems, technical controls, user reports, third-party reports, rights-holder reports, law enforcement requests, payment-risk signals, security signals, abuse signals, and other information.

TorBox may use automated systems to support account security, fraud prevention, abuse detection, copyright enforcement support, cache matching, de-caching, hash-based controls, rate limiting, API management, fair-use enforcement, spam prevention, malware or phishing detection, and service operations.

## 14. Not an Online Marketplace or Public Content Platform

TorBox is not designed to allow users to offer goods or services to consumers through TorBox. TorBox is not designed to publicly disseminate user content at a user’s request.

TorBox does not currently provide trader-facing marketplace tools, paid public storefronts, public product listings, public user profiles for content distribution, public recommender systems for user content, public advertising repositories, or public content feeds.

If TorBox changes the Service in a way that materially changes its DSA classification or obligations, TorBox may update this Notice and related policies.

## 15. Changes to this Notice

TorBox may update this Notice from time to time. The updated Notice will be posted on or through the Service and will be effective as of the date stated in the updated Notice, unless a different effective date is required by law.
