# TorBox Law Enforcement Request Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Law Enforcement Request Policy explains how TorBox handles requests from law enforcement agencies, government authorities, regulators, and similar public authorities seeking information related to TorBox, TorBox.App, our websites, applications, dashboard, APIs, software, tools, bots, integrations, support services, and related products and services (collectively, the “**Service**”).

This Policy is intended for law enforcement agencies, government authorities, regulators, and similar public authorities. Users should review the TorBox Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, Abuse and Illegal Content Reporting Policy, and Copyright / DMCA Policy for information about their rights and obligations.

## 1. Contact for Law Enforcement Requests

Law enforcement requests may be submitted to:

[legal@torbox.app](mailto:legal@torbox.app)

Emergency requests involving imminent risk of death, serious physical injury, child exploitation, terrorism, or other urgent safety matters should include the word “**Emergency**” in the subject line.

TorBox may require requests to be submitted through a verified government or law enforcement email address. TorBox may reject, delay, or request verification for requests submitted from personal email accounts, unverifiable domains, or unofficial channels.

## 2. Required Information

To help TorBox review a request, law enforcement should include:

1. the requesting agency’s name;
2. the requesting officer’s or official’s name, title, badge or identification number, and contact information;
3. the agency’s official email address and phone number;
4. the legal authority for the request;
5. a copy of the subpoena, court order, warrant, preservation request, emergency request, or other legal process;
6. the specific information requested;
7. the account, email address, username, API key identifier, payment identifier, IP address, URL, magnet link, info hash, file hash, filename, file size, NZB information, request identifier, timestamp, or other identifier relevant to the request;
8. the relevant date range;
9. the nature of the investigation, to the extent legally permitted;
10. whether user notice is prohibited by law or court order; and
11. any deadline for response.

TorBox may be unable to locate responsive information if a request does not include enough specific identifiers.

## 3. Legal Process Required

TorBox reviews law enforcement requests for facial validity, jurisdiction, scope, specificity, and consistency with applicable law and TorBox policies.

TorBox generally requires valid legal process before disclosing non-public user information. Depending on the nature of the information requested and applicable law, this may include a subpoena, court order, search warrant, production order, preservation request, mutual legal assistance request, or other legally recognized process.

TorBox may object to, challenge, narrow, or refuse requests that TorBox believes are invalid, overbroad, vague, disproportionate, unauthorized, inconsistent with applicable law, or otherwise improper.

## 4. Types of Information Potentially Available

Depending on the nature of the Service, account status, retention periods, technical systems, and the specific request, TorBox may have access to certain categories of information, such as:

1. account information;
2. email address;
3. account creation date;
4. account settings;
5. login information;
6. IP addresses;
7. device or browser information;
8. plan, subscription, voucher, billing, or payment metadata;
9. limited payment identifiers or payment processor references;
10. API key or access-token identifiers;
11. Download Requests;
12. URLs, magnet links, torrent information, info hashes, file hashes, file names, file sizes, NZB information, Usenet information, hoster information, or related technical metadata;
13. transfer metadata, cache metadata, queue status, transfer speeds, total transferred volume, and timestamps;
14. WebDAV, webhook, bot, RSS, cloud-export, or integration activity;
15. support communications;
16. abuse reports, DMCA records, enforcement records, or legal request records; and
17. other technical or operational records.

TorBox may not have the information requested. Certain information may be deleted, de-associated, anonymized, deidentified, overwritten, unavailable, technically inaccessible, or not collected.

## 5. Payment Information

TorBox uses third-party payment providers to process payment information and does not store full payment card numbers on its servers.

Law enforcement requests seeking complete payment card numbers, full payment credentials, bank information, or other payment-provider records may need to be directed to the applicable payment processor, bank, card network, wallet provider, subscription billing provider, reseller, or other payment provider.

TorBox may provide limited payment metadata in response to valid legal process where available and legally appropriate.

## 6. Preservation Requests

TorBox may preserve records in response to a valid preservation request where required or permitted by applicable law.

A preservation request should identify with specificity the account, user, API key, IP address, URL, magnet link, info hash, file hash, filename, file size, request identifier, timestamp, or other information to be preserved.

Preservation does not guarantee that TorBox has the requested information or that the information will be produced without valid legal process. A preservation request is not a substitute for a subpoena, court order, warrant, or other legal process required to obtain disclosure.

Unless a different period is required by applicable law or stated in a valid legal request, TorBox may preserve responsive records for a reasonable period and may require renewal or follow-up legal process to continue preservation.

## 7. Emergency Requests

TorBox may voluntarily disclose information to law enforcement or appropriate authorities if TorBox believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent death, serious physical injury, child exploitation, terrorism, credible threats of violence, or another emergency involving serious harm.

Emergency requests should include:

1. the requesting agency and officer information;
2. official contact information;
3. the nature of the emergency;
4. the specific information requested;
5. the specific account, user, technical identifier, or activity at issue;
6. why the requested information is necessary to address the emergency; and
7. a certification that the request relates to an emergency involving imminent risk of serious harm.

TorBox may require follow-up legal process after responding to an emergency request.

TorBox is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee immediate review or response. If you are aware of an emergency, contact appropriate emergency services directly.

## 8. User Notice

TorBox may notify users about law enforcement or government requests for their information unless prohibited by law, court order, or other legal restriction, or unless TorBox determines that notice would create legal, safety, security, abuse, enforcement, operational, or risk-management concerns.

If a request prohibits user notice, the request should include the applicable legal authority or court order.

TorBox may delay notice where legally permitted and appropriate.

## 9. Scope and Minimization

TorBox reviews requests to determine whether they are appropriately limited in scope. TorBox may seek clarification, narrowing, or modification of requests that are overbroad, vague, disproportionate, or insufficiently tied to the identifiers provided.

TorBox may disclose only information that it determines is reasonably responsive to the valid legal request, subject to applicable law, technical feasibility, and retention practices.

## 10. International Requests

TorBox is a global service with infrastructure, vendors, users, and administrative functions in multiple jurisdictions.

Law enforcement authorities outside the jurisdiction of the relevant TorBox entity may need to proceed through mutual legal assistance treaties, letters rogatory, local legal process, or other legally recognized channels.

TorBox may require international requests to be submitted through appropriate legal channels before disclosing non-public user information.

## 11. Costs

TorBox may seek reimbursement for costs associated with responding to law enforcement requests where permitted by law, including costs for searching, reviewing, preserving, collecting, processing, and producing records.

## 12. Confidentiality

Law enforcement requests and TorBox’s responses may contain confidential, sensitive, or private information.

Requesting authorities should protect information received from TorBox and use it only for the purpose authorized by the applicable legal process.

## 13. No Informal Requests

TorBox generally does not disclose non-public user information in response to informal requests, social media messages, community posts, support chats, phone calls, or other unofficial communications.

Law enforcement and government authorities should use the contact method listed in this Policy and provide valid legal process or emergency certification where applicable.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

TorBox may update this Policy from time to time. The updated Policy will be posted on or through the Service and will be effective as of the date stated in the updated Policy, unless a different effective date is required by law.
